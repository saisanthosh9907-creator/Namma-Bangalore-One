Empathy Mapping & Intervention

Bangalore One Queue Transformation Storyboard

Visualizing citizen pain points (confusion, time loss, anxiety) alongside proposed Design Thinking solutions written directly beneath each scenario.

1

Scene 1: Planning & Pre-Arrival

BEFORE (As-Is)

Arriving Blind & Unpredictable Crowds

"I took permission from my office for 30 mins... but the crowd spills onto the sidewalk!"

Peak hour rush • Zero visibility into queue length

Problem Statement: Citizens travel to centers without knowing live wait times, causing unexpected crowding and anxiety during office hours.
AFTER (To-Be)

Live Tracker & Virtual Tokens

"Indiranagar B1 shows 8 mins wait. I'll book my token right now from WhatsApp!"

Namma B1 App • Virtual Queue Spot Reservation

Design Thinking Solution: Real-time queue monitoring via WhatsApp bot or Namma B1 app lets citizens join the queue remotely and travel only when necessary.
2

Scene 2: Center Arrival & Triage Navigation

BEFORE (As-Is)

Confusion & Counter Hopping

"Counter 3 sent me to Counter 1, now Counter 1 says go to Counter 4!"

Unclear paper notices • No service categorization

Problem Statement: Unclear signage leads citizens to join wrong queues, resulting in frustration and staff overload from redirecting visitors.
AFTER (To-Be)

Smart Touch Kiosk & Color Micro-Navigation

"Kiosk printed my ticket: Counter 1 (Blue Zone). The floor lines lead right to it!"

Kannada/English Kiosk • Color-Coded Desk Floor Guidance

Design Thinking Solution: Self-serve dual-language touchscreen kiosk categorizes services instantly and assigns a color-coded ticket matching physical floor lanes.
3

Scene 3: Waiting Area Experience

BEFORE (As-Is)

Noise Fatigue & Anxiety Standing

"Was that number 45 or 54?! The speaker is distorted and I can't leave my spot."

Single display screen • Physical crowding in hallway

Problem Statement: Noise pollution and fear of missing one's turn forces citizens to cluster around counter desks, disrupting workflow.
AFTER (To-Be)

Proximity SMS Alerts & Digital TV Screen

"I sat comfortably reading news until my phone buzzed with an SMS alert!"

Haptic Push Alert • Clear LED Counter Display Boards

Design Thinking Solution: Automated SMS triggers alert citizens when their turn is 2-3 positions away, allowing them to relax seated or stay outside nearby.
4

Scene 4: Counter Service & Payment

BEFORE (As-Is)

Missing Papers & Slow Cash Change

"You didn't bring a bill photocopy? You must go outside to copy and rejoin the queue!"

Transaction aborts • Cash change delay (4-5 mins/person)

Problem Statement: Missing document requirements discovered at the counter force complete restarts, frustrating both staff and citizens.
AFTER (To-Be)

Pre-Verification Safeguard & Express UPI QR

"Checked my documents on app beforehand! Scanned QR and paid via UPI in 45 seconds!"

Digital Pre-Check Checklist • Rapid Counter QR Reader

Design Thinking Solution: Interactive pre-check checklist prevents issuing tokens without required paperwork. Express UPI QR reduces payment time by 80%.
